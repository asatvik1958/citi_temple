Sri Venkateswaraswamy Temple, Kallakuru

TEMPLE HERITAGE

Nestled in the serene village of Kallakuru, the Sri Venkateswaraswamy Temple stands as a spiritual landmark and a testament to unwavering devotion. The temple, dedicated to Lord Venkateswara – the preserver and protector, has become a sacred pilgrimage center for devotees across the region. The temple’s spiritual journey began with the tireless efforts and devotion of local villagers and well-wishers who envisioned a space where faith, culture, and tradition could flourish. After years of collective dedication, rituals, and auspicious ceremonies, the temple gradually evolved into the divine space it is today.
